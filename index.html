FAQ

Questions coaches ask first

Can I bring my current Google Sheet?

Yes. Send us your clients’ sheets and we move the clients and their data into Vari8 for you.

Do I have to learn a new system?

Your tracker keeps the columns you use now and the calculations you choose, so the day-to-day looks familiar. What changes is the rest: your clients get an app, and messages, photos and check-ins sit in one place.

Do my clients need to install an app?

We’re building a dedicated client app for the App Store. How clients get in during early access is still being decided.

What will it cost?

Pricing isn’t decided yet. Joining the early access list doesn’t commit you to anything.

Who is Vari8 for?

Coaches who need more than a basic fitness tracker. You decide the fields, calculations, plans and check-ins, for prep and lifestyle clients alike.

What happens to client data when I change things?

Changing a view, plan or template doesn’t delete anything. Logs, plans and check-in history stay with the client.
